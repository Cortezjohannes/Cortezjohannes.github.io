What does “Daddy of Agentic Gaming” mean?

It is Johannes’s tongue-in-cheek name for a serious thesis: AI should create new forms of play, not just add generated content to yesterday’s games. Agentic gaming is still an emerging field with no single established originator. Johannes is one of the founders advocating for it through Prom13us, Mochi and Sitrep.
